CryptoIntelligence Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CryptoIntelligence handles information when you use the app. CryptoIntelligence helps you organize cryptocurrency portfolio information and watchlists and view market, discovery, news, and public-data information. It is not an exchange, broker, custodian, wallet, financial adviser, or trade-execution service.

In this policy, “we,” “us,” or “our” means Esfandiar Esfandiari, the developer and provider of CryptoIntelligence.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

CryptoIntelligence stores information you create and app state in the app’s local container. This may include:

  • portfolio holdings, quantities, cost basis, currencies, and manually entered prices;
  • cash balances;
  • portfolio account labels, provider selections, website links, and notes;
  • watchlist items, reasons, notes, and review dates;
  • decision-review notes and theses; and
  • discovery, monitoring, research, market snapshot, and preference information.

CryptoIntelligence has no CryptoIntelligence-operated account or backend system and does not use CloudKit or iCloud synchronization. This does not mean that all information stays on your device: network-backed features send the limited request information described below.

3. Information Sent to External Services

Some features send information needed to retrieve market, project, repository, governance, blockchain, news, or other public data. Depending on the feature, a request may include:

  • an asset, project, or repository search term;
  • an asset symbol or provider identifier;
  • a project, protocol, repository, or governance-space identifier;
  • a public blockchain network, address, or contract identifier; and
  • normal network information such as IP address, request time, destination, and protocol metadata.

An asset symbol or provider identifier from a locally stored holding may be sent to retrieve its current price. These requests do not send portfolio quantities, cost basis, cash balances, account labels, wallet or exchange labels, watchlist notes, personal theses, or decision notes.

4. External Data Providers

CryptoIntelligence uses third-party market, research, repository, governance, blockchain, news, and public-data services to provide requested information. Representative services include CoinGecko, GitHub, DefiLlama, blockchain and governance data providers, and news publishers. The providers used may change as the app’s sources evolve.

These services receive the request information needed to respond, together with normal network metadata such as your IP address and request/service-use information. Some providers state that they process access or service-usage logs, search or interaction data, and technical metadata for purposes such as service delivery, security, rate limiting, analytics, personalization, or service improvement, according to their own practices. Other providers do not publish equally specific information.

External services operate under their own privacy practices. CryptoIntelligence does not control their server logs, retention, subprocessors, international processing, or response to privacy requests.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

CryptoIntelligence offers subscriptions that are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes your payment information; CryptoIntelligence does not receive your payment-card or banking information.

The app uses StoreKit-provided product, subscription, and entitlement information — such as which product you selected and whether your entitlement is current, expired, or revoked — as necessary to determine your access to paid features. CryptoIntelligence does not operate its own subscription backend and does not persist a separate purchase-history database; Apple and StoreKit remain the system of record for your subscription.

Restore Purchases uses Apple’s StoreKit synchronization mechanism to re-check your current entitlements with Apple. Restoring does not create a separate CryptoIntelligence account or a developer-operated purchase-history service.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

CryptoIntelligence does not include a developer-operated analytics SDK, third-party crash-reporting SDK, advertising SDK, attribution SDK, App Tracking Transparency request, cross-app or cross-site tracking implementation, or data-broker integration. The app does not display advertising.

These statements describe the current app. Apple may separately receive App Store or operating-system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. External data providers may maintain request logs or conduct their own analytics under their policies; that processing is described in Sections 3 and 4.

7. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the app is used to:

  • organize, display, and calculate locally stored portfolio information;
  • maintain watchlists, discovery state, monitoring, and decision notes;
  • retrieve current market, project, news, repository, governance, and public blockchain information;
  • cache results and display snapshots or research evidence;
  • diagnose request failures locally; and
  • present purchases, restore access, and determine subscription access through StoreKit.

8. Data Sharing

CryptoIntelligence sends the limited request information described in Section 3 to external services when necessary to provide a network-backed feature. CryptoIntelligence has no CryptoIntelligence-operated user database, advertising integration, or data-broker integration.

External providers control their own processing and may use service providers or disclose information as described in their policies. Purchase and entitlement information is exchanged with Apple through StoreKit as needed to provide, verify, and restore the subscription service.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored information remains in the app container until you delete a supported record through available app controls, the app replaces or expires particular cached information, or the app container is removed. The app does not define one fixed retention period for every local cache or history record.

External-provider retention is controlled by each provider under its own policies. CryptoIntelligence does not know or promise a particular provider’s retention period or remote deletion.

10. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can delete supported individual records, including holdings, cash balances, portfolio accounts, and watchlist items, through available app controls. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local application container, subject to Apple’s device backup and restore behavior.

CryptoIntelligence has no account or backend, so there is no CryptoIntelligence account-deletion flow. Uninstalling the app does not delete information that an external provider may have independently logged from prior requests. Questions about a provider’s information should be directed through that provider’s own privacy-request process.

For questions about CryptoIntelligence privacy practices, contact cryptointelligence.support@gmail.com.

11. Security

CryptoIntelligence relies on Apple’s app sandbox and platform storage mechanisms for local app data. External requests use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

12. Children’s Privacy

CryptoIntelligence is not directed to children.

13. International Processing

External providers and their service providers may process requests in jurisdictions other than your own. Their locations and processing practices are governed by their respective policies.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features, providers, or applicable requirements change. The published policy will identify its effective date. Where appropriate, changes may be communicated through the app or https://esfand11.github.io/.
